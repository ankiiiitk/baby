A letter for you

Less sparkle for a moment. Just me, being honest with you.

My dearest ,

I know you're upset with me, baby, and I am sorry. I don't want to guess at your feelings, minimise what happened, or act as though a cute website can make everything disappear.

Even I am not always able to understand my own emotions well. I am trying to make sense of them, and I want to be honest with you about that. I am not saying this to seek sympathy, or to make you responsible for how I feel.

I don't want to be controlling. I want you to feel free, respected, and loved—not managed. Yes, I want to be vulnerable with you, to let you see the parts of me I don't always know how to explain. But I am not opening up this way with everyone else; this kind of honesty is something I want to share with you.

You don't owe me instant forgiveness. Take the time you need. When you're ready, I want to listen without defensiveness, understand what matters to you, and make things right through what I do—not just what I say.

I love you, baby. I am sorry—and I want to do better, with you and for us.
